About me

Bridging purpose and partnerships: across international development, government contracting, and nonprofit leadership. I'm a strategist known for spearheading multimillion-dollar international portfolio growth while strengthening the organizational infrastructure that makes growth last, and a changemaker who loves cultivating high-value partnerships and connecting strategic vision with purpose-driven implementation.

Today I'm the Founder & CEO of Azuvila, a Woman-Owned Small Business advising small businesses and mission-driven organizations on strategic growth, business development, capture and proposal strategy, and nonprofit fundraising and capacity. Before founding Azuvila, I spent twelve years at Bixal, where I helped build an international development practice from the ground up and grow a global portfolio to roughly $100M across USAID Missions on three continents.

My path started as a Rural Community Development Volunteer in the Peace Corps in Costa Rica, where I learned in practice that people are the key to success and that partnerships only work when they're connected around a shared purpose.
