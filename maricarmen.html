About me

For nearly two decades I've worked at the intersection of strategy, partnerships, and purpose — spanning government contracting, international development, and nonprofit leadership. I love the work of turning big missions into durable partnerships and real, measurable growth.

I'm the founder of Azuvila, a Woman-Owned Small Business that advises small businesses and mission-driven organizations on strategic growth, business development, and partnership strategy. In my day-to-day, I lead strategic growth and partnerships at Bixal, a mission-driven company delivering digital services and international development work for government.

My path started in the Peace Corps as a Rural Community Development Volunteer in Costa Rica (2006–2008). That experience shaped everything that followed — a belief that the right people, connected around a shared purpose, can move mountains. Today I stay close to that community through board and advisory roles, and I hold an Executive Master of Arts from the Thunderbird School of Global Management.
